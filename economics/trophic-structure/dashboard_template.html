Trophic Networks of 30 Economies

The hidden plumbing of 30 economies. Each sector is placed by its trophic level — how many steps of production separate it from raw inputs. Pick a country, hover any sector, and watch where its output sits in the chain.

—

Vertical position = trophic level (raw inputs low, final-facing high). Node size = gross output · inner pink = self-flow · outer green = throughflow · ring colour = production community · arrows = the heaviest inter-sector flows.

Hover a node for details. The 12 biggest sectors are labelled.

How to read a node

● outer green disk — throughflow (gross output)
● inner pink disk — self-flow (sector buying its own output)
ring colour — the production community a sector belongs to
height — trophic level: deeper in the supply chain = higher up
